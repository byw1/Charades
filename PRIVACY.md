# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

Deckhead is a party game that works entirely on your phone. This policy is
short because there is very little to say.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Deckhead has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking and no servers. It never sends anything about you or your games
anywhere.

## What stays on your phone

The decks you make, your games in progress and your settings are stored on your
device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Sharing a deck

When you share a deck, the whole deck is packed into the QR code, link or file
itself. It goes only where you send it — straight to the person you share it
with — and never passes through anyone else's server.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — only to scan a deck's QR code, and only when you choose to. No
  photos or video are taken or stored.
- **Motion** — only for the optional tilt controls, to tell when you tip the
  phone to answer. Nothing is recorded.

## Children

Deckhead collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/byw1/Deckhead/issues>.
