## What this means for forks

You may fork, modify and redistribute the code under AGPL-3.0. If you do, you
must rename.

**Forks must:**

- Choose a different app name, and not one confusingly similar to Charades: Make Your Own Decks
- Replace the icon and any wordmark with their own
- Change the bundle identifier from `com.charades.app` (and any ID this project ships under)
- Change the URL scheme from `charades://` and `deckhead://`
- Not present the fork as an official release of this app

**Forks may:**

- State factually that the work is derived from Charades: Make Your Own Decks, for example "based on
  Charades: Make Your Own Decks" or "a fork of Charades: Make Your Own Decks", provided this does not suggest endorsement
  by or affiliation with the copyright holder

The intent is not to restrict the code. It is to keep users from installing
something they believe is this app when it is not.
